# Design System & Visual Direction — Clinicore

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design World
**Minimal Clinical Professional** — A serene, high-trust medical interface optimized for clinical operations, rapid scanning, and data clarity.

## Color Palette
- **Backgrounds:** Clean white (`#ffffff`) and soft cool near-white surface (`#f8fafc` / `#f1f5f9`).
- **Primary Accents:** Deep clinical navy (`#0f172a` / `#1e293b`), calm medical teal (`#0d9488` / `#0f766e`), and subtle slate blue (`#3b82f6` or muted `#2563eb`).
- **Semantic Status Colors:**
  - Success / Good: Calm green (`#10b981` or `#059669`)
  - Warning / Pending: Muted amber (`#d97706` or `#f59e0b`)
  - Critical / Urgent: Controlled clinical red (`#dc2626` or `#e11d48`)
- **Neutral / Text:** High contrast dark slate (`#1e293b`) for primary text, muted slate (`#64748b`) for secondary labels.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Clean, high-legibility sans-serif (`Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, sans-serif).
- **Hierarchy:** Strict proportional scaling, clear font weights (regular `400`, medium `500`, semibold `600`), optimized for dense tables, schedules, and medical records.

## Layout & Data Density
- **Spacing & Structure:** Generous white space around containers, tight and clear alignment within data tables and cards.
- **Borders & Shadows:** Clean 1px subtle borders (`#e2e8f0`), no heavy shadows, no glows, no glassmorphism.
- **Buttons & Badges:** Solid or cleanly outlined buttons with sharp or moderately rounded corners (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`), absolutely no gradient fills or gradient text.

## Strict Bans (Anti-Patterns)
- **NO** purple/violet gradients or neon accents.
- **NO** glassmorphism or backdrop blur overlays.
- **NO** bouncy or distracting animations.
- **NO** large decorative icon tiles above headings.
- **NO** gray text on colored backgrounds.
